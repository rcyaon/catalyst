A catalyst doesn't change what's possible. It makes it happen sooner. We're Olin's student-run hub for entrepreneurship: funding, mentorship, and a community of builders for turning ideas into real things.

The elements of Catalyst.

Everything the club does, in one table. Pick an element to see what it is.

Grants of up to $1,000

Prototype parts, a domain, user-interview gift cards, a booth at a market. If money is the thing between you and the next version of your project, ask us for it.
